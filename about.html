Our Story

We've helped over 5,000 animals get rescued since 2005. Our nonprofit currently has over 100 volunteers making sure our animals are comfortable and able to find new homes. We proudly maintain over a 98% successful placement rate between owners and their new furry friends.

Our Core Values

  • Compassion

    Every animal is treated with kindness and care.

  • Commitment

    We do our best to make sure every animal here at our rescue finds the perfect home.

  • Community

    We lean heavily on our amazing community to help adopt, foster, and get the word out about our animals.

Our Partners

We aren't able to do this work alone. Our partners provide resources, services, and help support us care for all of our animals.
